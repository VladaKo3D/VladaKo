GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know before we start creating together.

How much does a 3D product animation cost?
Pricing depends on the product, animation length, complexity, and required deliverables. Share your project details and I'll prepare a custom quote.
How long does a project take?
Most projects are estimated at around 5–14 working days, depending on their scope and complexity. Larger-scale projects may take longer.
Do you offer revisions?
Yes. The number of revision rounds is agreed upon before the project begins, so expectations are clear.
Do I need to provide a 3D model?
Not necessarily. I can create a product model from suitable photos, dimensions, and references. Existing 3D files are also welcome.
Can you create vertical videos for social media?
Yes. I can prepare animations in vertical, horizontal, or square formats depending on where you plan to use them.
How do we get started?
Send me a short description of your product, reference images, and what you'd like to create. I'll review the requirements and discuss the next steps with you.
